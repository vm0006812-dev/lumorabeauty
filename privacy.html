Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes how the Lash Room By Mika content tool (“the app”) handles information. The app is a private tool operated by Lash Room By Mika for its own TikTok account.

What the app does

The app lets the account owner connect their own TikTok account, prepare their own photos and videos, and publish them at scheduled times.

Information we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (such as display name), provided through TikTok Login Kit.
  • An access token that TikTok issues so the app can publish content on the owner’s behalf.
  • The photos and videos the owner chooses to publish.

How information is used and stored

Information is used only to publish the owner’s content to the owner’s TikTok account. Access tokens and media files are stored on the owner’s own computer. We do not upload them to any other service and we do not keep a separate database of users.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Content is sent only to TikTok, as requested by the owner, in order to publish it.

Visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or forms, and it does not collect information from visitors.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Stored tokens can also be deleted from the owner’s computer.
